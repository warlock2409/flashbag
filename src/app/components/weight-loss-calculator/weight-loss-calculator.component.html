2 · Diet vs training — what each one decides

A calorie deficit is what makes you lose weight — so why train at all? Because the deficit decides whether the scale moves; training decides what you actually lose.

Think of it like moving house

You need to throw out 20 kg of stuff. Without instructions, movers might toss your sofa, TV and laptop along with the old newspapers. A diet alone leaves your body guessing the same way. Strength training is the instruction: “keep the muscle — burn the stored fat instead.”

Burning ~300 kcal in a session is one of the least important things training does. The bigger wins:

3 · Losing 10–15 kg in 90 days

90 days = 13 weeks. One kg of fat stores ≈ 7,700 kcal, so the daily deficit you need scales with the goal:

10 kg fat loss
10 × 7,700 = 77,000 kcal
≈ 850 kcal deficit / day
15 kg fat loss
15 × 7,700 = 115,500 kcal
≈ 1,280 kcal deficit / day
Program target deficit
750 – 1000 kcal / day
✅ Diet control ✅ Strength training ✅ Walking / cardio ✅ Protein intake

Important: we never take daily intake below 1,500 kcal. Losing ~1 kg/week needs a ~1,100 kcal/day deficit, which for many people would fall under that floor — so your real safe maximum depends on your own numbers. See your personal safe maximum ↓
